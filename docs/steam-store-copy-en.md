### 💬 FAQ

**Q: How is this different from Wallpaper Engine?**
A: Wallpaper Engine is a wallpaper platform where you find/create your own. Danqing Pomodoro is a complete focus tool — scenes, audio, timer, stats — all ready out of the box.

**Q: Is my data uploaded anywhere?**
A: No. All data is stored locally in `%APPDATA%/danqing/` as plain JSON. View, export, or delete anytime.

**Q: Does it support multiple monitors?**
A: You can drag the window to any monitor. Multi-monitor pinning is planned for a future update.

**Q: Will more scenes be added?**
A: Yes. New scenes will arrive via free updates or DLC.
